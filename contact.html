FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us in the form above.

Which tools does Arthos connect to?

Arthos works in both directions. It reads from wherever your knowledge lives, such as wikis, documents, and websites. Your other tools, including AI assistants and chatbots, can then query Arthos for current, reliable facts.

How long does setup take, and what do you need from us?

You can start querying Arthos within the first week, but setup continues throughout the pilot. Once you share the documentation you want Arthos to track, it starts building your knowledge graph right away. Over the following weeks, we refine it with you so it reflects what matters most to your team. You choose how to connect: through our API or the Arthos UI.

Do we need to replace any of our current tools?

No. Arthos works alongside the tools you already use.

How is our data protected?

Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and Arthos doesn't access it without your explicit permission. Read more about security.

What happens after the pilot?

We'll decide next steps together. You can end the pilot with no further commitment, or become a fully onboarded Arthos customer.

How much does it cost?

Partner pricing is available on request. We'll work out terms that fit your team.
