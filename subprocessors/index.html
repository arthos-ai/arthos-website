Subprocessors

Last updated November 2026

Arthos uses a small number of third-party companies, called subprocessors, to help deliver our service. This page lists every subprocessor that may process customer data on our behalf.

Current subprocessors

Subprocessor Purpose Location
Amazon Web Services Hosting and data storage United States
OpenAI Large language model (LLM) processing; no training on customer data United States
Anthropic Large language model (LLM) processing; no training on customer data United States
Google Workspace Email, file storage, and video calls (including Google Meet) United States
Slack Team communication United States
Zoom Video calls United States

Changes to this list

We'll notify customers before a new subprocessor begins processing their data, and we'll update the date at the top of this page.
