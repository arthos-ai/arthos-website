Privacy policy

This policy covers this website, arthos.ai, only. It does not cover the Arthos product itself, which design partners use under a separate agreement with its own data-handling terms.

What we collect

  • When you use our contact form, we collect what you submit: what your message is about, your name, email, company, and message.
  • We use Google Analytics to understand how people use our site. It collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, your approximate location, and your device and browser type.
  • Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. Some pages include embedded YouTube videos, and YouTube may collect information when you play one.
  • Our hosting provider may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • We use your form submission only to read and respond to your message.
  • We use analytics information only to understand and improve our site.
  • We don't use any of it for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

  • We use Formspree, a third-party form service, to store form submissions and deliver them to us by email. Formspree handles this information under its own privacy policy.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, Google Fonts, and YouTube, and handles the information they collect under Google's privacy policy. Cloudflare hosts this site.
  • We don't share your information with anyone else.

Cookies

Google Analytics and embedded YouTube videos use cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form submissions for as long as we need them to respond to you and keep a record of our conversation. If you'd like us to delete your information, contact us and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time — just reach out through the contact page or at hello@arthos.ai.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business use and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us through the contact page or at hello@arthos.ai.
