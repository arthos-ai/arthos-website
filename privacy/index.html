Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy covers this website, arthos.ai, only. It does not cover the Arthos product itself, which design partners use under a separate agreement with its own data-handling terms.

What we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to submit through our contact form: what your message is about, your name, email address, company, and the message you write. We don't use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking on this site. Our hosting provider may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

How we use it

We use what you submit solely to read your message and respond to you — for example, to follow up on a design partner inquiry. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

We use Formspree, a third-party form service, to store form submissions and deliver them to us by email. Formspree handles this information under its own privacy policy. We don't share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form submissions for as long as we need them to respond to you and keep a record of our conversation. If you'd like us to delete your information, contact us and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time — just reach out through the contact page or at hello@arthos.ai.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business use and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us through the contact page or at hello@arthos.ai.
