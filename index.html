Where this shows up

A few places knowledge quietly stops agreeing with itself.

Regulatory & ordinance changes

A city updates a zoning or safety code, and different departments' procedures haven't caught up with each other or with the new rule.

M&A integration

Two merging companies' HR, accounting, and ops policies say different things about the same question, and no one's reconciled them yet.

Franchise continuity

A franchise spanning multiple writers, years, and formats accumulates details that quietly contradict earlier canon long before a fan or reviewer catches it.
