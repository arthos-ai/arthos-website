How it works

How Arthos keeps your organization's knowledge connected and current.

Here's what Arthos does with the knowledge inside the tools you already use.

The process

From scattered sources to one current answer.

Arthos is a trusted knowledge layer. It sits on top of your existing tools, so nothing gets migrated or replaced. It works in four steps.

01Extract

Arthos connects to the documents, wikis, tickets, and systems your teams already use. It pulls out the individual facts inside them. Each fact links back to where it came from and when it was recorded.

02Connect

Arthos maps how those facts relate to and depend on one another. That way, a change in one place can be traced to everything built on top of it.

03Check

As sources change, Arthos shows what else a change has left out of date. It also flags where one fact has replaced another, or where two sources disagree. Your team sees the evidence and decides what's correct.

04Publish

The current knowledge is available to your teams and your AI tools. Every fact comes with its source, so anyone can check where it came from.

This isn't a one-time cleanup. Arthos runs continuously. As your sources change, it shows what each change affects and flags what no longer fits.

What this changes

Fewer surprises for your teams and your customers.

Your customers get one consistent answer.

When something changes, every place a customer looks reflects it. They get the same answer from your website, your support team, and your chatbot. That builds trust and cuts down on corrections.

Know what a decision affects before you make it.

Before a change goes out, you see what it affects and which teams need to act. Problems get caught before they break something. So teams spend less time redoing work.

Every tool works from your latest decisions.

Your AI tools draw on the policies and decisions your team has approved, not an outdated document a search happened to find. Every answer traces back to its source. So if something looks wrong, you can see exactly where it came from.

Catch problems while they're small, not after they're expensive.

When two sources disagree, you see both versions side by side, while it's still easy to fix. Your team resolves it once, in one place, before it reaches your customers or becomes a risk for your organization.

Who Arthos is for

Built for organizations where one team's decisions shape another team's work.

Arthos is for organizations where knowledge lives across many systems. Plans, policies, and decisions change often. And what one team decides determines what other teams build.

The common thread is dependency. Every team creates knowledge that other teams rely on. But it's hard to see who depends on what. A team making a change can't easily tell who it affects. And the teams it affects often don't know anything has changed. A change that goes unnoticed doesn't stay contained. It reaches other teams, what ships, and your customers.

Arthos makes those connections visible. When something changes, the team making it can see who will be affected. And the people, workstreams, and AI tools that depend on that knowledge see the change and work from the current version. Catching a change early costs far less than catching it late.

The more sources you have, the faster your knowledge changes, and the more teams depend on it, the more this matters.

Regulatory changes

A city updates a safety code. Every department procedure built on the old rule now has to change, and each team needs to know.

Mergers and acquisitions

Two companies merge, and their HR, finance, and operations policies answer the same questions differently. Every decision about how to combine them affects work across both organizations.

Franchise continuity

A story spans many writers, years, and formats. A change to one detail affects everything written after it, long before a fan or reviewer notices.

Questions

A few questions that tend to come up.

Is this a search tool?

No. Search tools help you find documents that match a question. Arthos works on the facts inside those documents. It keeps track of how they connect and what depends on them. So you can see what a change affects and work from the current answer.

Do we have to move our documents or replace our tools?

No. Arthos sits on top of the tools you already use. Your documents stay where they are, and your teams keep working the way they do today.

Who decides what's correct?

Your team does. Arthos shows what a change affects and the evidence behind each version. People make the call.

Is this a one-time cleanup?

No. Knowledge keeps changing, so Arthos runs continuously. When something changes, you see what it affects right away, not months later during a review.

How does this help with our AI tools?

Your AI tools can draw on the same current facts your teams use. Every fact comes with its source, so answers can be traced back and checked.

Is Arthos only for large organizations?

It depends less on size and more on how your knowledge works. The more sources you have, the more often things change, and the more teams depend on each other, the more Arthos helps.

Do you provide the interface our team uses?

Either way works. We can provide the interface, or fit Arthos into the tools your team already uses.

Who owns the data we share with you?
