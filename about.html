Why we started Arthos.

We spent years leading AI, product, and engineering teams at one of the world's largest companies. We kept seeing the same problems across different parts of the organization:

  • A flagship AI product had thousands of people behind it and still couldn't keep its answers current.
  • A large support organization spent close to a third of its people just keeping content up to date.
  • A push to make teams more productive with AI stalled on knowledge gaps.

The problem wasn't effort or tools. Nothing kept knowledge current as information changed. So we started Arthos to do that.
