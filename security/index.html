Security at Arthos

Arthos is built to handle your organization's most important knowledge. Here's how we protect it.

Encrypted end to end

All customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit, including backups.

Respects your permissions

People only see what they can already access in your tools.

Never trains shared models

Your data is used only to produce your team's results.

Hosted in the US

Arthos runs on AWS in the US East (Ohio) region.

Data protection

  • All customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Arthos is hosted on AWS in the United States (US East – Ohio).
  • Each customer's data is logically isolated from every other customer's.

Access follows your permissions

  • Arthos respects the permissions in your existing tools. If someone can't see a document in your organization today, they won't see it through Arthos.
  • Customers can sign in with SSO and MFA, and roles control who can do what within Arthos.

How we use AI

  • Each customer's data is used only to produce that customer's results. It is never used to train or tune models shared with other customers.
  • We access AI models from OpenAI and Anthropic through their APIs, under terms that do not allow your data to be used to train their models.

Arthos employee access to your data

  • Arthos employees cannot access customer data without your permission.
  • You control whether Arthos support can access your data, and any access is logged.

Backups and deletion

  • Backups are encrypted and stored separately from production systems.
  • When a contract ends, customer data, including backups, is automatically deleted within 90 days. Customers can request an export of their data before then.

Data ownership

  • Your data stays yours. This includes the information you provide to Arthos and the facts and insights Arthos derives from it, which we'll return to you on request.
  • Arthos owns its software, methods, and the internal structures it uses to organize your data, such as metadata.

Incident response

If we confirm a security incident affecting customer data, we'll notify affected customers without undue delay, and within 72 hours.
